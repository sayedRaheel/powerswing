# Mission: Understand the powerswing POC well enough to defend it

## Why
Raheel built (with Claude) a POC that predicts H100 power drops and simulates smoothing. He needs to explain it to investors and possibly turn it into a research paper, so he must understand the data, the methods and the limits well enough to answer hard questions without notes.

## Success looks like
- Explain in 60 seconds what the dataset is, what we predicted, and what the result means in dollars
- Answer a skeptical reviewer's question about the data (where drops come from, how many independent jobs, what 5 Hz misses)
- Explain why an always-on limiter can match the ML, and when prediction still wins
- Read any chart in reports/figures and say what it shows

## Constraints
- Wants both a simple view and a deep view of every topic
- Non-native English writer; plain words, no em dashes
- Time-poor founder: short lessons, one idea each

## Out of scope
- Writing the training code himself
- General ML theory beyond what this project uses
