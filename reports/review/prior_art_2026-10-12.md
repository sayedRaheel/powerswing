# Prior-art check (2026-10-12)

Independent research-agent review. Abstract-level unless noted. Verify before citing.

## Closest work
- EasyRider, arXiv 2604.15522 (v2 2026-09-11, "Submitted to E-ENERGY 2027"). Rack hardware (LC filter, DC-DC, LiFePO4 battery), reactive, no workload forecast. Same 10%/s ramp target. 19% less energy than software burn. Notes software-triggered batteries can take up to 1 s to detect a ramp. Does not use NLR data.
- arXiv 2609.22542, Lyu et al. (2026-09-18). Battery smoothing with a model-based predictor (phase advance + Taylor) that only compensates 5 to 20 ms comms delay. Uses the NLR dataset (one profile). Up to 94% max-ramp reduction. MUST cite.
- arXiv 2606.04869. HESS + residual differentiable predictive control, perfect 0.64 s preview, synthetic workloads, grid side only, "forecasting errors are not considered".
- arXiv 2508.14318 (Microsoft/OpenAI/NVIDIA). Software burn, GB200 floor/ramp, rack storage. 90% floor = 10.5% energy. Prediction only named as future work.
- arXiv 2609.13490 (active filter, reactive), 2512.08076 (battery + supercap, no forecast).
- Forecasting without mitigation: 2503.07756 (IEEE PES GM 2025, peer reviewed), 2605.04074 (5 to 80 min, MIT Supercloud V100), 2605.00681 (distillation, MIT Supercloud).
- Context: 2604.10769 (workload mix vs ramps), 2606.22096 (resonance / iteration period), 2603.18383 (inference trace generation).
- Industry: NVIDIA GB300 power smoothing, Oracle AET smoother (reactive ms-scale fill).

## Novelty of our contributions
- C1 seconds-ahead forecast + drop early warning with lead time and false alarms/hour: appears novel in specifics; generic GPU power forecasting exists. Beating an FFT/ACF baseline on periodic traces is a modest claim.
- C2 learned forecaster with real errors arming fill/limiter, with actuator latency and energy vs violation trade-off: partially done (predictive control exists with oracle or delay-only predictors); our specific combination not found.
- C3 "no ML needed if actuator is instant" + desynchronized site never violates: appears novel as an explicit design rule.
- C4 leave-one-workload-out failure: appears novel, low weight alone.
